---
title: JuliaGiac
---

JuliaGiac brings [Giac](https://www-fourier.univ-grenoble-alpes.fr/~parisse/giac.html),
the computer algebra system by Bernard Parisse and Renée De Graeve, to
[Julia](https://julialang.org/): exact symbolic algebra, calculus, equation
solving and linear algebra, with more than 2,000 Giac commands, conversions to
Symbolics.jl, SymPy.jl, MathJSON.jl and Nemo.jl, and typeset output in
notebooks.

```julia
using Pkg; Pkg.add("Giac")
```
